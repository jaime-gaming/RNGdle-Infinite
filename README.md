# RNGdle Infinite

**Roll a number from 0 to 1,000,000. Find out what makes it special.**

RNGdle Infinite is a browser game about rolling numbers, earning EP, collecting
badges and buying upgrades. No energy meter, no daily limit, no account. **[▶ Play it](https://jaime-gaming.github.io/RNGdle-Infinite/)** — works on
desktop and mobile.

![The roll screen](media/roll.png)

## The basics

1. **Roll.** Press **GENERATE**. You get a random whole number from 0 to
   1,000,000.
2. **Score.** The number earns EP from its patterns (pairs, runs, lucky sevens
   and more). Each pattern it has is a **badge**.
3. **Wait.** A short reveal and a cooldown keep things paced. Upgrades make
   both shorter.

Your EP is your wallet. Spend it on upgrades, companions and skills to roll
faster and earn more.

![A finished roll with its badges](media/result.png)

## Ways to grow

- **Companions.** Thirteen friends that multiply the EP you earn. Wear one at a
  time; you can find some for free while you roll.
- **Skills.** Charged effects that fire on your next roll: double EP, extra
  draws, no cooldown and more. Skills never change how a number is scored.
- **Tasks and badges.** Goals to chase and a collection to fill.
- **Rebirth.** Start over with a permanent bonus.
- **Prestige.** A bigger fresh start, once you have half your collection and
  30 million EP earned in the cycle.
- **Rollback.** Appears after three prestiges, and you can repeat it.

### RNGween World

Speak to **The Reaper at the foot of Roll**, beside the footer. World has **four phases**, each themed around an event aura; entry is public, saved rolls need a profile. Auras still come from the original missions, not World rolls.
**Pumpkin Patch (1–9):** a lantern-lit start, 8 badges and saved collection milestones. **Graveyard (1–99):** collect **5 badges**, earn **150 event points** and get **Six Mark 3 times** for the first fragment.
**Haunted House (1–999):** collect **7 badges**, earn **1,000 event points** and roll **Rare+ 10 times** for the second. Free rolls, stacking badges, no resets on common results.
Forge a free key from **one fragment of each kind**, or buy the **2,000 Pumpkoin** shortcut. It opens **Valley of Horrors** until expiry and is never consumed.
The Valley's saved **1–9999** pool never repeats through reloads, linking or resets; older saved numbers also restore their event badges. Every **6** digit is red; **6666** alone earns permanent, never-sold **Hexbound**.
The original SVG Reaper has a compact sketch portrait, short typewritten dialogue and typed/selected replies. World has a direct `/rngween-world/` Pages entry. Decorated reveals start at **10s**. The internal shop sells sequential **Wisp Lantern (200 coins → 8s)**, **Phantom Clock (500 → 5s)** and **Reaper's Hourglass (1,000 → 2s)**.
Speed applies to future rolls, never odds or ordinary Roll timing. Pending outcomes/deadlines are saved before animation; reloads cannot reroll them.
All **39 World badges** and their points are event-only. Per-zone last/best results and collections save independently. World rolls give no ordinary EP, charges, drops or task/aura credit; event progress expires, owned cosmetics stay.

Main rolls cap at **six numbers**; nonfitting skills stay charged. Each multi-roll
keeps its aura inside its number panel. The mobile rack **folds** and remembers
your choice; the first visit shows a once-only event explanation.

**Oct 21–24 & Nov 1–3:** four hourly commissions from a pool of **50**, no skips.
Finishing pays Pumpkoins and immediately deals a new task; expiry pays nothing.
Only online play counts. World tasks work in the public badge hunts without a key;
one shared board persists through reloads, device linking and resets.
Each task gets one hour, but all stop at the aura-week/event boundary.

## Your progress

Your save lives in your browser. To play on another device, open
**Settings → Device link** on one device and open the link (or scan the QR code)
on the other. Anyone with the link can play your account, so keep it private.

Once two devices are linked and both are open, they stay in sync. The device
that created the link is the main one: it runs rolls and purchases, so the two
screens never fight over the same roll.

## FAQ

**Is there a roll limit?** No. Roll as often as you like once the reveal and
cooldown finish.

**Does a companion make my rolls luckier?** No. Companions multiply the EP you
earn. Your number and its badges stay the same.

**Is this the official RNGdle?** No. It is an independent recreation. See the
credits below.

## Run it locally

```sh
npm install
npm run dev     # http://localhost:5173
npm test        # the Playwright suite (needs a Chromium binary)
npm run build   # production build
```
