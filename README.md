## How to play

1. **Roll** a number from 0 to 1,000,000.
2. **Discover** the patterns and curiosities that make it stand out.
3. **Collect EP** and spend it on upgrades, skills, companions, and auras.
4. **Keep going:** tasks, badges, and fresh starts give every run something to chase.

![A number revealed with its badges](media/result.png)

## What you can discover

- **Badges** for patterns, famous numbers, and mathematical curiosities.
- **Companions** that boost the EP you earn.
- **Skills and upgrades** that change the pace of a run, never the odds.
- **Daily and weekly tasks**, a long-term badge collection, and new milestones.
- **Auras** to change the look of your game, plus a history of your rolls.

## Battle Pass

The Battle Pass runs in 28-day seasons, each one named and themed after the
month it opens in; the first is **First Light**. Every pass mission — daily,
weekly and season milestones — lives on the **Tasks** page, and pays **BPEP**,
which only moves the pass; EP remains your shop wallet. The Pass page shows the
vertical reward track across 30 levels, plus the consumable boosts that levels
5, 15 and 25 hand out (one charge a day can also be bought with EP). The optional premium track is bought with EP, never real
money. Earned rewards are kept; unclaimed earned rewards arrive when the season
closes. Rebirth never resets the pass, and the cosmetics you claim are yours
forever.

## Black Friday 2026

On **November 27, 2026 (00:00–24:00 UTC)**, the shop offers product-specific
20–50% discounts. Spending accumulates across purchases: every 100,000 EP spent
during the event earns a stamp. At 25 stamps, the permanent Blackout 2026 aura
is unlocked. Missing RNGween looks are also available on the event shelf for
10,000,000 EP each. The sale does not change roll odds or scoring.

The event shelf, posters, temporary themes and sale branding are available only
on the day. Event information is shown in advance; earned auras and their
history remain after the event.

## Keep your progress

Open **Profile → Continue locally**, then give your local profile a name to keep your progress on this device. A Cloud Account is optional if you want to continue on another device. If you play offline, progress is saved locally and a Cloud Account syncs again when you reconnect.

You choose whether to share a public profile. A profile logo appears only when that profile is shared. To go back to an earlier saved copy, open **Settings → Restore save**; you can check the copy's progress before choosing it.

![Choose a saved copy to restore](media/save-recovery.png)

Local saves are stored with lossless compression, so a longer activity history fits on this device without deleting recovery copies. If saving still fails, keep your site data and free space before trying again.

## FAQ

**Does anything change the odds?** No. Every number has the same chance, and companions or upgrades never improve your luck.

**Can I choose a number?** No. Each roll is random; repeats are part of the game.

**Can I play offline?** Yes. Your named local profile keeps progress on this device; cloud progress syncs after you reconnect.

**What happens to old event rewards?** Rewards already earned remain in your collection and activity history.

**Is this the original RNGdle?** No. This is an independent game inspired by the original.
